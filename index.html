Command Reference

🟡 = perm required
🎵 Music 13 commands
/play <query>
Play a song or YouTube playlist by name or URL. Auto-joins your voice channel.
/stop
Stop playback, clear the queue, and disconnect from voice.
/skip
Skip the current track and advance to the next in queue.
/pause
Pause playback without losing your queue position.
/continue
Resume a paused track. Alias: /resume.
/resume
Alias for /continue — resumes paused playback.
/replay
Restart the current song from the very beginning.
/repeat
Toggle repeat mode on/off. Loops the current track indefinitely.
/nowplaying
Shows current track title, duration, requester, and repeat status.
/queue
View the full music queue including what's currently playing.
/shuffle
Randomly shuffle all tracks currently in the queue.
/clearqueue
Wipe all songs from the queue without stopping playback.
/volume <0–100>
Set playback volume. Applies instantly to currently playing audio.
